## Problem

*Acquit* is a single-player card game based on the O.J. Simpson murder trial. The player is
Simpson's defense attorney and tries to prove his innocence using **evidence**, **witnesses**, and
**credibility**.

On a team of 6, I designed the gameplay systems: the **scoring mechanics** and the **cards**
themselves. The challenge was making a courtroom defense feel like a strategic card game, where
evidence, witnesses, and credibility interact in ways that are fair and interesting.

## What failed

**I overscoped the design.** The original plan let the player take **both** sides of the case,
prosecution and defense. Two complete sets of mechanics had to be fun on their own *and* balanced
against each other, and I kept running into balance problems I couldn't solve in the time we had.

## What I changed

I cut the prosecution side and focused the game on **one role: the defense**. With one side, the
mechanics could be designed and balanced as a single system.
